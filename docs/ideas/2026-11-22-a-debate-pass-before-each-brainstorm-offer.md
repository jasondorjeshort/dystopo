# A debate pass before each brainstorm offer

Source: coordinator (claude-opus-5-5), after the triage of 2026-11-22 (the second offer, after proposal 0065 and ADR 0065); offered and accepted by the owner; not sanity-checked.

Before the owner sees an offer, a second, cheap agent argues one line against each candidate, and the offer shows both. It is the one finding the brainstorm research supports (Nemeth et al. 2004: debate instructions beat no-criticism brainstorming) and a check on the coordinator's sycophancy (Sharma et al. 2023).

Lever: proposal 0065's critic found six serious problems in the coordinator's first framing of this very practice. Outranks the stats idea: quality before volume is the order the owner chose.

**What exists already (to be checked):** ADR 0065's offer has the cheap grep check only; the critic step exists for proposals, not offers.

Question: Should every brainstorm offer pass through a cheap critic agent first, with its one-line objection shown beside each candidate, as an amendment to ADR 0065's offer (decisions 1 and 2)?
Recommendation: A, as a short ADR 0065 amendment plus a carry-back of triage step 9 and wave Close step 6; it is the one practice ADR 0065's own research (Nemeth et al. 2004, Sharma et al. 2023) supports, and a Sonnet-class critic over nine lines costs little against the owner's reading time. It reverses nothing the owner chose, but it adds an agent to every offer, which the owner set free of cost guards.
Options:
A. A Sonnet-class critic on every offer, one objection line under each candidate.
B. The critic on moonshots only (where the coordinator's framing is least checked).
C. Not now: keep ADR 0065's grep check alone; revisit when the stats idea can show what the owner keeps.
D. Not worth building.

Answer: A, a Sonnet-class critic on every offer (the owner, 2026-11-22, multiple choice).
