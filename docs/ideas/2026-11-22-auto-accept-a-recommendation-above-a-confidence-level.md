# Auto-accept a recommendation above a confidence level (moonshot)

Source: coordinator, generated by Opus 5.5 and recorded by Opus 5.5, after the ideas triage of 2026-11-22; offered and accepted by the owner; not sanity-checked. Moonshot.

At the 2026-11-22 triage the owner took the recommended option on 19 of 20 multiple-choice questions. The
owner, reshaping the offer: "The recommendations are good but that one "nay" was significant. What we could
do is figure out some sort of confidence level above which we auto accept." The one departure (the phone's
sweep timing: all three parts, but at most once a week) changed the scope, so a plain streak count per
class of question would have missed it.

The shape: each `Question:` block carries the coordinator's confidence in its recommendation (with what
drives it: settled by an ADR or a survey, reversible, user-visible, safety, new data flow); questions above
a threshold are decided by the coordinator and reported, the rest are asked. The record of picks against
recommendations (the ideas-stats tool) calibrates the threshold. Anchors: the triage skill's step 6,
`docs/ideas/README.md` (the block format), ADR 0065, `.claude/skills/triage/SKILL.md`.

The owner, later the same day: "okay that one nay wasn't that significant this time. but in a lot of ideas
that are design for the documents, the expert user is going to have much better input than an agent will."
So the kind of question weighs more than the streak: design of the project's own documents and process is
where the owner's input beats the agent's, and belongs below the threshold whatever the record says.
