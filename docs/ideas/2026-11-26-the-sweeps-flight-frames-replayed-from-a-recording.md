# The sweep's flight frames replayed from a short recorded window

Source: the triage's agent, generated by claude-opus-5-5 and recorded by claude-fable-5-2, a triage; offered and accepted by the owner; not sanity-checked.

The sweep's frames that show flights (the map with flights, the Horizon view and the Panorama with their flights
layer) differ from sweep to sweep, because the aircraft are wherever the replay or the feed puts them at that moment;
the before-and-after sheet then shows a change that is not one. Wave 48's T365 built a recorder for flight polls
(devtool) and left two two-hour recordings.

The idea: a short window cut from such a recording becomes a fixture (a public area, Rainier), and the sweep's flight
steps replay it from a fixed start through the app's existing replay route (`app/aircraft/AircraftPoller.kt`,
`POST /aircraft/replay`), with the clock the frames read pinned to the window, so the same aircraft stand in the same
places in every sweep.

Lever: flight frames stop moving in every release's sheet, and the guessed-position work to come can be judged by eye
against a fixed scene.
