# In direct mode, a large area offered as several downloads, each started by a tap

Found while planning wave 48 (the coordinator's, from a read of the public vector source's terms; not the owner's
words). ADR 0060's owner paragraph ruled out "a split that only evades a per-area limit". The terms as fetched on
2026-11-24 (`https://openfreemap.org/tos/`, by a read-only agent) now say, under Offline Downloads: "Larger areas or
long routes can be saved as several separate downloads, each started by the user and each within these limits."
The limits are "at most 3,000 tiles and at most 50 MB" a download, "at most 4 parallel requests" and "a unique
User-Agent identifying your app".

**Where it stands.** Wave 48's cap task refuses a direct-mode download over the cap with a sentence that offers a
smaller area, less detail or a server; it offers no parts. A 1 degree box at 47° N is about 4,600 vector tiles, so a
day's hiking area can already be over; nearly every country row is.

**What could be built.** Over the cap, the Download card shows the area cut into parts that each fit, and the user
starts each by its own tap ("Part 2 of 4"); the parts work together for search and routing as separate downloads
already must (ADR 0060). A long route's corridor the same way.

## Open

- Whether a tap per part is within the terms' spirit when the app drew the parts; the owner's email to the source's
  operator (Owner actions pending) could ask it outright.
- How many parts are still reasonable to tap (four, ten), and what the card says beyond that (a server).
- Whether the cap's sentence should name the parts as its first offer.

Question: build the parts offer, each part by its own tap, now that the terms name it?
- (a) Recommended: yes, as a pass after wave 48's cap has merged, up to about six parts, a server offered beyond.
- (b) Ask the source's operator first, and build on a yes.
- (c) No: the refusal with its three offers is enough.

Answer: (a), yes, after wave 48's cap has merged (the owner, 2026-11-24, by multiple choice).
