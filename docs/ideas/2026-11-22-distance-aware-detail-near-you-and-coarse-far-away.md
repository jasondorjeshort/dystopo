# Distance-aware detail: fine near where you go, coarse far away

Status: **untriaged and not sanity-checked** (an idea of the coordinator's own, offered when the owner asked for more and recorded on the owner's word, 2026-11-22). Nothing here has been read against the code or measured; the anchors and the list of what exists are from memory of the session's reading, to be checked at triage.

A map keeps itself fine near where the owner is, goes, or has saved routes, and coarse far away: a pack thins by distance from home, from tracks and from saved routes, so the budget follows the use.

**What exists already (to be checked):** **Related ideas exist:** areas that update themselves by use (`2026-11-04`) and auto-download when zoomed in (`2026-10-13`); not the thinning of a held map.
