# `--check-first` says when a name's frames will be left out of the before-and-after sheet

Source: the triage's agent, generated by claude-opus-5-5 and recorded by claude-fable-5-2, a triage; offered and accepted by the owner; not sanity-checked.

`python -I tools/sweep-diff.py --check-first <names>` says which part and step save each name's frame and exits 1 when
the sweep runs none (T319). It does not say whether those frames will reach the before-and-after sheet that the owner
reads at a release: a frame can be saved by its step and still be left out of the sheet (the closing sweep's follow-up
of 2026-11-26). A new screen is then in the sweep and absent from what anyone looks at, and nothing says so.

The idea: `--check-first` also reports, per name, whether its frames are in the sheet's set and, if not, the rule
that leaves them out; a name the sweep saves and the sheet drops is a warning line and a field in the JSON.

Lever: five tasks of wave 49 add screens, each checked by `--check-first` alone.
