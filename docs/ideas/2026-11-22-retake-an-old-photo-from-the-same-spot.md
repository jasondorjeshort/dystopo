# Retake an old photo from the same spot, at the same light (moonshot)

Status: **untriaged and not sanity-checked** (an idea of the coordinator's own, offered when the owner asked for new ideas after the triage of 2026-11-22, and recorded on the owner's word). Anchors are from the session's reading, to be checked at triage.

Old photos, the one clock and the full-screen lens together: the app guides you to where an old photo was taken, shows when the sun stands where it stood, and lays the old photo over the lens so the two line up for a then-and-now picture.
