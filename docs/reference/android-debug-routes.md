# Android debug routes

Debug builds only, on the debug port (`android-app.md`, Loopback token). The KDoc at the top of each `*Routes.kt` file is the full reference for its routes; this page is the map. Tracks and sync: `android-tracks-and-sync.md`; direct sources, updates and first run: `android-online.md`.

## Endpoints

Debug endpoints (debug builds only, on the debug port; `curl -H "X-Dystopo-Token: $T" http://localhost:<forwarded port>/...`). They run on their own small thread pool, so they answer while contour tiles generate:
- `GET /health`: packs, server load (`rejected` counts per reason, tile and debug pool queues), map state. `map.idle` means style loaded, MapLibre's `isFullyLoaded`, last frame complete, no tile pending at the server, no camera move and no new frame for 500 ms; `map.notIdleBecause` names the blockers (`styleLoading`, `animating`, `pendingTiles`, `notFullyLoaded`, `frameNotFullyRendered`, `rendering`, `settling`); `map.lastScreenshot` says how the last screenshot settled.
- `GET /screenshot[?wait=ms][&mode=window][&stable=0]`: PNG of the map (MapLibre snapshot). It waits for idle and then until two snapshots in a row agree, so it never returns a half-loaded map; `stable=0` skips the pixel check, `wait=0` captures at once. `mode=window` copies the map surface and draws the Compose overlays on top. **View it**, like the devtool PNGs.
- `GET /camera`; `POST "/camera?lat=46.853&lon=-121.76&zoom=12&bearing=0"` (or a JSON body; like any camera move that is not the app's own it stops following the fix, and a bearing other than north turns north-up into free rotate). T93, T100 (`controls/ControlRoutes.kt`): the map has one orientation state for the whole app (`controls/MapOrientation.kt`): `north-up` (the default), `free` (entered only by turning the map with two fingers) or `facing` (the map turned to the heading), kept across restarts as north-up or facing, plus `following` (the camera stays on each fix; a recentre turns it on, a one-finger pan, a fling or a move that is not ours turns it off; two-finger turns and pinches act about the centre and keep it). Navigation's camera modes (`/nav/camera`, the navigation card's Recenter, Heading up and North up) are a view of the same state, so the card, the button and the follow-me agree. `POST /camera/recenter[?animate=ms][&taps=n]` does what `n` taps (1 to 5) on the recentre button (lower right) do, by the owner's rule: off centre a tap recentres on the fix and keeps the orientation; on a centred map it advances it (north-up to facing when the phone has a compass, facing and free rotate to north-up); it answers once the camera is there with the camera, `fixOffsetPx`, `orientation`, `tap` (`action` `recenter` or `advance`, `centredBefore`, `orientationBefore`, `orientation`, `advanced`, `navFollowOn`) and `taps` (409 with `noFix` or `noPermission` when there is no fix). `POST /camera/orientation?mode=north-up|free|facing` sets the orientation; `POST /camera/heading?deg=` sets a simulated heading for facing while fixes are injected (no `deg` clears it; the same one as `/nav/heading` and `POST /location?bearing=`). `GET /camera` adds `fixOffsetPx`, `orientation` (`mode`, `following`, `centred` and its `centredTolerancePx`, `cameraMode`, `compass`, `heading` with `deg`, `source` `simulated` | `course` | `compass`, the course, the speed and the compass sensor's state, `lastChange`, `lastFollowEnd`, counters) and `recenter`: the button's `state`, `orientation`, `lastTap`, and `button` (`boundsPx`, the credit, route card, track panel and navigation card edges, `clear`, and `icon`: the orientation and look it drew, `following` blue, `offCentre` dark, `disabled` grey; T106: `tripCardTopDp` and `updateCardTopDp`, the trip card's and the update offer's top edges, which it now rides above; it hid under the trip card and shared its corner with the update offer). Facing's heading is the course over ground while moving (faster than 1 m/s), else the compass (rotation vector plus WMM declination, running only while facing is on and the app is in the foreground). On the emulator the compass follows the virtual sensors: `adb emu sensor set acceleration 0:0:9.81` (lying flat) and `adb emu sensor set magnetic-field -20:0:-45` (pointing magnetic east; `-B sin H : B cos H : -45` for heading H) settle in about 10 s; a replayed GPX (`POST /location/replay`) gives the course.
- **Scale bar** (T101, `controls/ScaleBarRoutes.kt`): `GET /scalebar` says what the bar lower left shows (`shown` or `hiddenBecause`; T106: it rides above the trip card and the update offer, `bottomCardsTopDp`, instead of hiding under the trip card; `units`, `bothUnits`, `night`, the `zoom` and `lat` it was fitted at, `metersPerDp`) and per bar (the user's units first) `label`, `meters`, `widthDp`, `widthPx` and `drawnPx`, where it sits (`boundsPx`, the credit line, route card and track panel edges it stays above, `clear`), and `readBack`: each bar against MapLibre on the live camera (`mapLibreMetersPerDp`, the metres that width covers at it and `errorPct`; `measuredMeters` between two screen points that far apart across the centre and `measuredErrorPct`). `POST /scalebar?both=on|off` is the Map options row "Scale bar" (One unit, Both units: a two-sided ruler, the other system below the line); a device setting, not synced.
- `GET /stats`: tiles served per source, contour generation ms (min/avg/max, recent tiles with sample/trace split, `warmup`), disk-cache hits, DEM decode cache, memory (Java heap, native heap, PSS by category), APK and pack bytes.
- `POST "/location?lat=..&lon=..&accuracy=10"`: inject a fake GPS fix (the location puck moves; device fixes stay detached until `POST /location?device=1`); `GET /location`.
- `GET /settings`; `POST "/settings?units=m&textScale=1.4&warmup=0"`: units (`ft`, `m`, or `unset`), text scale (0.5 to 3; outside is a 400), contour warm-up; `server` (the online tile server, empty for offline only; T79: plain http only to a home-network address, see First run). Cache caps: `onlineCacheMb` (fetched online tiles) and `contourCacheMb`, one LRU byte cap enforced while the app runs over every pack's contour tiles, the cliff-rewritten vector tiles (`cliffs1-<vector id>-<dem id>/`) and the hillshade/slope shade tiles (`shade1-<options>-<dem id>/`); a deleted pack's directories go with it. T74: online tiles are keyed on the server's content versions (`GET /version` there, `X-Dystopo-Version` on tiles; checked at most every 15 s on a tile request and by the probe), so a server whose DEM changed is seen at the next check and its old tiles (and the contours and shade made from them, which are cached in the online cache too) are dropped (T95: except the `dem` and `osm` tiles of pinned offline areas, kept at the version each area recorded until it is updated; Areas of interest below); glyphs and sprites follow the server's `/assets/manifest.json` hashes (`/online` `assetsVersion`, `assets`); the fetcher takes a list of sources (`/online` `fetcher.sources`). Map options (T47, also Settings › Map options): `hillshade` off/single/multi, `hillshadeIntensity`, `slope` off/avalanche/steepness (T104, `slopeChoices`; `on` is avalanche; the bands and legend in `GET /shade` `slopeLegend`, docs/PLANNING.md › Slope shading), `contourInterval` auto or a value from `contourIntervalChoices` (from contour zoom 11, pack and online contours alike), `contourLabels` sparse/normal/dense, `trails`, `water`, `landcover`, `pois`, `boundaries`, `night`; the default style is unchanged unless an option needs shade tiles (`/shade/{z}/{x}/{y}.png`, `/stats` `shadeGeneration`; T104: `packFloorReads` counts online shade tiles read at a DEM pack's first zoom, `blankAtPackFloor` those still without data, which should stay 0). Units are a preference (docs/PLANNING.md › Units): unset on install, seeded once from the first pack's centre or the first fix, then only the user changes them; contour tiles, the style, track stats, the profile and search results follow them. `POST /settings/ui?open=1` opens the settings sheet (also the Settings chip on the map; `open=0` closes it). T103: the sheet opens on a list of groups and a tap opens a group's page (side by side on a window 720 dp wide or more); `GET /settings/layout` lists the groups (`id`, `title`, `summary`) and each one's rows in page order (`label` as drawn, `drawnBy`, `since`), the group with the server field (`serverGroup`) and what is shown now (`open`, `group`, `twoPane`); `POST /settings/open?group=data|map|display|navigation|sync|about|list` opens the sheet on a group's page (or the list) for screenshots and tours.
- **Storage** (T74, `storage/Storage.kt`; Settings › Data and storage › Storage ›): `GET /storage[?walk=1]` lists every store with bytes and budget: `packs` (`files/packs/` and pushed ones), `online` (`cache/online/tiles/`, fetched tiles plus online contours and shade, `onlineCacheMb`), `derived` (`cache/contours/`: pack contours, shade, cliffs, `contourCacheMb`), `sources` (`cache/sources/`, the phone's own source reads); `walk=1` adds each directory's `files`, `apparentBytes` and `diskBytes` (compare with `adb shell du -s -k`). `POST /storage?onlineCacheMb=&contourCacheMb=&pinnedCacheMb=` sets the budgets (the same settings), `POST /storage/clear?store=online|derived|sources` empties one, `POST /storage/ui?open=1` opens the screen. T91: the tiles of pinned offline areas are a store of their own, `pinned` ("Offline areas (pinned)", budget `pinnedCacheMb`, default 2048 MB): `online` and `onlineCacheMb` count only unpinned tiles (`allBytes` is both), and an area download whose manifest would take the pinned bytes over the budget fails with a message naming both numbers (an area downloaded again does not count its own tiles twice). The budget chips wrap instead of scrolling (the 2 GB chip was clipped at the right edge). T95: each row's `holds` and `entries` count its own tiles (the online row listed the pinned ones too, so it read 0 MB over a dem line with an area pinned); the `pinned` row has `holds` per kind and `olderThanSource`, the names of pinned areas whose source has moved on to a newer version (T106: or that were fetched from another source than the one set now), shown as "Older than the source: <names>. Update in Offline areas."
- `POST /reload` (reopen packs after a push), `POST /cache/clear` (drop cached contour tiles).
- `GET /features` (alias `/labels`): rendered feature count per style layer in the viewport (queryRenderedFeatures) plus placed symbols per symbol layer; `GET /features/text`: pixels that change when text is hidden (0 with labels placed = labels not drawn).
- `GET /contour-labels[?minRunPx=200]`: index contours in view and which carry a placed label; unlabelled ones with their visible length and longest on-screen run (`labelledLong`/`indexLong` count lines with a run of at least `minRunPx`).
- `GET /packs`: `active` (the primary pack), `inUse` (every pack on the map with `primary`, `demRank`/`osmRank` in precedence order, `osmProfile`), `roots`, and per pack `name`, `active`, `usable`, `bytes`, `bounds`, `minZoom`/`maxZoom`, `attribution`, `dem`/`vector` (file, bytes, tileType, zooms, bounds, tiles, attribution, title, `complete`), `files`, `errors`, `inUse`; plus `downloads`, `search` (per-pack index state) and `routing` (the trail graphs: the primary pack's state at the top, every pack's under `packs`, the graph tile cache, the last route's `memory`). Precedence per tile (`PackPrecedence`): outdoors profile over plain OpenMapTiles, then the primary pack (`POST /packs/activate?name=`), then the deeper archive, then the smaller box. `DELETE /packs/{name}` removes the archives, the search index, the routing graph and (on the next collection) the pack's contour, cliff and shade tiles. `GET /attribution`: full credits plus `mapLine`, what the on-map credit line shows for the packs overlapping `viewBounds` (and the online sources when the view is online).
- **Routes** (T45, `RouteRoutes.kt`): `POST "/route?from=46.7864,-121.7366&to=46.8024,-121.7289&mode=hike"` plans over the trail graphs of every pack near its ends (T77: with a server set, an end beyond every pack on the device is planned by the server's `GET /route` instead, `memory.remote` in the route, `remote` in `GET /route/status`; keep it with `POST /routes/keep`, then `POST /areas/download?trip=<id>` fetches its corridor so navigation works offline; drives of 40 km or more use the drive overlay, `driveOverlay` in `/route/status`) (T76: each built on first use into `<vector archive>-graph.idx` next to its pack, z11 graph tiles read on demand, so a route crosses from one pack into the next; progress, tile cache and the last route's `memory` in `GET /route/status`) and shows it, fitting the camera to it; results carry `via`, the named trails in travel order (outdoors packs; plain OpenMapTiles lines have no names). `GET /route` (card state, `ui.lastFit`, `ui.layout` with the route card and track panel edges and `overlap`), `POST /route/mode?mode=bike` (answers with the replanned route; T73: `mode=drive` follows the packs' roads, one-ways and roads closed to cars, says on the card what drive does and does not do (`Router.DRIVE_NOTICE`, ADR 0032), and on a pack built before outdoors profile 2 fails with `drive_unsupported` naming the pack to rebuild), `/route/longpress`, `/route/save`, `/route/gpx`, `/route/export`, `/route/clear` (T65: also closes the route editor, so a kept route's line goes too; the route stays stored, `closedTrip`). T65: a route has one set of numbers everywhere: the card, `route` in `POST|GET /route`, the kept route, the trip card, the Routes list and navigation all show `RouteSummary` of its points (T24's 3 m hysteresis climb, the T45 hike model); the router's graph-based climb and time are under `route.graph`. `POST /route/import/pick` opens the Routes list and the system file picker (its Import button; pick a GPX, it is imported as routes and the last one opens), `GET /route/import` has the result. Trips (planned routes, `/routes`) are documented in `trips/TripRoutes.kt`; the trip card has Go. T106: a drive kept before T89 (routed legs with no router time, so the flat 45 km/h estimate) says so on the trip card with Re-plan; `GET /routes/{id}` has `untimedDriveLegs` and each leg's `timeS`, `POST /routes/{id}/replan` is Re-plan. T108: `mode=crow` (here, `/route/mode`, `/routes` and `/routes/{id}/mode`) is a straight line between the points with the DEM pack's heights, no graph and no snapping (`route.bearingDeg`, one arrival in `instructions`; the card has a Crow chip and the line "A crow route is a straight line between the points, not a path to walk."); a trip keeps its directions (the `directions` sync field: Keep saves the plan's, a new trip and every edit save the editor's, legs an edit left alone keep theirs), so `GET /routes/{id}` `directions` (`saved`, `count`, `shown`, `text`) and the trip card's "Show N directions" work with no graph and after a sync; a trip saved without them gets them in the background when opened where a graph covers it (`directions.working`, `GET /routes` `directionsMigrated`; `POST /routes/{id}/open?wait=1` answers after it, `POST /routes/{id}/directions?save=1` makes them now, `?open=1|0` opens the card's list), else the card says they were not saved. A trip in a mode this build does not know shows it plainly (`modeLabel` "walk (unknown mode)") and refuses edits with a reason (400). The route not yet kept outlives the process (`files/route-session.json`; `GET /route` `ui.restored` after a restart).
- **Waypoints at any time** (T91; `TrackRoutes.kt` documents the routes, `tracking/LongPressMenu.kt`): the map's Waypoint chip shows folded or not, recording or not; `POST /waypoint[?lat=&lon=][&name=]` does what it does: a waypoint at the given point or at "here" (the recorder's last fix or the map's last device fix, whichever is newer; 409 with neither), on the recording track while one records, else free-standing, and the waypoint sheet opens to name it. Free-standing waypoints sync like any other and draw whatever track is shown (`GET /tracks` `freeWaypoints`, `lastDrop`). A long-press on the map now opens a card, "Waypoint here" (elevation from the DEM pack), "Route from here" (the T45 start) or Cancel; while a route waits for its end the press goes straight to the route, so planning is long-press, Route from here, long-press. `POST /map/longpress?lat=&lon=` is that long-press, `POST /map/longpress/waypoint|route|cancel` taps the card, `GET /map/longpress` its state; `POST /route/longpress` still feeds the route directly.
- **Navigation** (T61, T65; `NavRoutes.kt` documents every route): `POST /nav/start?route=current|<kept route id or prefix>|<saved route>` (or `track=`, `file=`, a GPX body), `GET /nav`, `GET /nav/status`, `POST /nav/stop`, `/nav/camera`, `/nav/heading`. Starting closes the route editor and hides the route card; the navigation line draws above the planned, kept and track lines (`layer.overlaysAbove` empty). `POST /nav/snooze?min=10|until=back|off=1` silences the off-route alert (events are still listed; `snooze.suppressed`); the banner has Snooze 10 min and Quiet until back. Stale fixes: a GPS fix or the last known location more than 30 s old is dropped (`fix.droppedTooOld`), the last known location more than 2 km from the line does not start navigation (`droppedTooFar`), and with no usable fix for 30 s the bar says "No GPS fix for N s" (`fix.lost`). T91: those thresholds and the banner's snooze are settings, `POST /settings?navStaleS=30&navStartM=2000&navSnoozeMin=10` (5 to 600 s, 100 to 50,000 m, 1 to 240 min; read when navigation starts; `fix.maxAgeS` in `/nav/status`), and two rows of Settings › Navigation and tracking (stale after 15, 30, 60 or 120 s; Snooze 5, 10 or 30 min). `etaVsPlan` is a pace ratio, (fix time since the first fix + the model's time still to go) / the planned time (the route's `RouteSummary` time; the line's model time for tracks): 1.0 at the start, above 1 behind plan; `pace` has the parts. `layout` says where the Tracks and Routes chips sit: they stack below the stats card (`TopChips`), and the Routes chip sits below the open tracks list. Off the device: `./gradlew :devtool:run --args="nav replay --path shared/src/commonTest/resources/gpx/skyline-loop.gpx --path-track --fixes shared/src/commonTest/resources/gpx/skyline-offroute.gpx --expect-off 1 --expect-arrive"` runs the same off-route logic on the JVM (events, max distance, progress, `etaVsPlan` as JSON; exit 1 when an expectation fails). T102, turn directions: a route carries `instructions` (in `POST|GET /route` under `route`; descriptions and failure messages follow the units preference), the route card offers "Show N directions" (the default detail level, priorities 1 and 2; `POST /nav/directions?open=1|0`, `directions` and `directionsOpen` in `GET /route`), and `GET /nav/directions[?route=current|<kept or saved route>][&level=1|2|3]` lists a route's directions without navigating (each with `inDistance`, "In 300 m, turn left onto ..."). Navigating, the card shows the next direction with its distance and a turn arrow, purple within its lead distance; `/nav/status` lists `instructions` and `nextInstruction` (`text`, `distanceM`, `distance`, `withinLead`) and `passed`, and `GET /nav` has `directions` and `ui.turn`. A kept or saved route reads its directions from the packs on the device (only depart, via points and arrive without one); `devtool nav replay --pack` shows the same on the JVM. T108: a kept trip's saved directions come first (`source.directions` `saved` in `/nav` and `/nav/directions`, else `packs`), so a trip synced from another phone navigates with them offline and with no pack.
- **Areas of interest** (T75; `areas/`, routes in `AreaRoutes.kt`, the manifest format in docs/SERVER.md › Areas of interest): a box or a route corridor with zooms, pinned on the phone. A box is "Download this region" (`RegionDownload`, through the server's `POST /area` when it has one, so the pack arrives with SHA-256 per archive; recorded as a pack-backed area, which stays until removed); a corridor (the trip card's Offline button, or `POST /areas/download?trip=<id>[&bufferM=500&minZoom=8&maxZoom=15][&contours=1]`, or a JSON body `{"kind":"corridor","line":[[lon,lat],...],"bufferM":500}`) fetches the manifest's DEM (from z6, or z8 with no server) and vector tiles through the online fetcher into the tile cache, skipping tiles in packs or already cached (a second run resumes), and pins them: `TileCache.keep` (one hook line in `online/TileCache.kt`) is `Areas.isPinned`, a spatial test of each key's z/x/y against the pinned covers, so eviction skips them until `POST /areas/{id}/pin?on=0`. T91: a pack-backed area (a box) has no Pin or Unpin: its card says "KEPT AS A PACK" and why (a pack is never evicted), and `POST /areas/{id}/pin` on it is a 409 with the same words. A trip's corridor defaults to z8 to z15, and z8 to z13 for a drive (an 84 km drive was 134 MB at z15; `maxZoom=` overrides). The checks bring a route in as a trip with `POST /routes/import` (a GPX body; `trips/TripRoutes.kt`), then `POST /areas/download?trip=<id>`. `GET /areas` (each area's bytes, estimate, coverage now: tiles present out of the manifest's), `GET /areas/{id}[/manifest]`, `GET|DELETE /areas/download`, `DELETE /areas/{id}`, `POST /areas/trim?mb=N` (cap the online cache now, not saved; `restore=1`; answers evictions and every area's coverage before and after), `POST /areas/ui?open=1` (the "Offline areas" sheet, also a chip in the pack list; outlines on the map while open). Ahead on the route: while navigating and online, the next 3 km of the line (1 km segments, 300 m buffer, z12 to z15) are fetched and pinned until navigation stops; `GET /areas/prefetch` lists the segments with `aheadByM`, `secondsBeforeArrival` and `cachedAtArrival` (`POST /areas/prefetch?on=0` turns it off). T95, versions: each area records per source the content versions its tiles were fetched at (`versions` in `GET /areas`); when the server's version changes (any `/build`, a new planet) the area's `dem` and `osm` tiles are kept at that version and served from it, online and offline, until the area is refreshed (`olderThanSource` names what moved on; `coverage.atOlderVersion` counts tiles found only at the area's version; `/online` `fetcher.pinnedKept`, `pinnedHits`, `unheldDropped`). Update on the area card (shown instead of "Download again" with "Older than the source. Update downloads the newer data.") or `POST /areas/download` for it again fetches the current version, records it, and deletes the old tiles no other area holds (`refreshDropped` in `GET /areas/download`); the prefetch ahead refreshes its segments the same way (`derivedDropped`). `POST /areas/plan` (the download's parameters) answers the corridor's manifest size, tiles per route and `fits`/`refusal` against `pinnedCacheMb` with nothing started; the trip card's Offline button shows that first (`POST /routes/{id}/offline`, then `?go=1` for Download or `?cancel=1`; `trips/TripRoutes.kt`), and a download the budget refuses records nothing. T106: `GET /areas` has `fromOtherSource` per area (fetched from a source the phone no longer reads: then `olderThanSource` names what it holds, the card says "Downloaded from another source" and Update fetches it from the source set now and deletes the old source's tiles in its cover), `pinnedBySource` (pinned tiles and bytes per source cache key, `inUse`) and `outline` (the sheet's corridor polygons: a corridor's band is its outline filled once, not a line as wide as the buffer, which folded into wedges at bends); `POST /areas/{id}/retry` and `POST /areas/{id}/dismiss` are the card's Retry and Dismiss on a failed, cut short or refused download (Dismiss keeps what is on the phone, `done` or `partial`, or removes an area with nothing downloaded, `removed`); an unpinned area stays unpinned until the budget lets its Update go on; the corridor line follows the units setting. `POST /online/cache/clear[?pinned=1]` keeps the pinned tiles unless `pinned=1` (`deleted`, `pinnedKept`).
- **Sync**: the `/sync/*` and `/share/*` routes are in the Sync and Phone-to-phone sync paragraphs below; the share listener is a separate server with its own share code (T72: 16 characters, the link encrypted under it), not the loopback token.
- `tools/android-probe.py --port <port> --serial <serial>` sends malformed and oversized requests and checks the server answers 400/408/413/414/431 and stays up, that requests without the token (or with a wrong one) get 401, and (T57) that `/health` without the token is 401 on the debug port and that device port 8686 serves map routes only (debug routes 404).
- T57 also: `/stats` tile counters are cumulative since the app started (`counters.tileServiceOpens`), not reset when a pack install, a synced setting or `/reload` reopens the tile service (T65: `shadeGeneration` and `cliffs` too); `POST /settings?routeSnapM=50..1000` (default 300; the settings sheet offers 300 m, 500 m, 1 km) is how far a route's ends may be from a trail, and an off-network failure names the nearest trail, its distance and direction (`nearestName`, `nearestDirection`); `/route/status` has `decoder`; `POST /packs/catalog/refresh[?server=]` fetches the catalog again (T65: a server's vector-only single-file pack is listed and downloads as a map-only pack, `<name>-outdoors.pmtiles`, drawn over another pack's or the online DEM and never primary); `POST /location/replay?record=` is a 409 while a track is recording (replay without `record=` adds to it).
