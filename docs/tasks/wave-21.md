### T149. No whole-pack graph or search build behind a download or at launch, and a graph build bounded by the heap
**Goal:** the routing-graph finding's Next. (1) `RegionDownload.recordArea` stops starting `PlaceSearch.ensure` and `RoutePlanner.ensure` on its `area-products` thread; routing already builds on first use (`RouteSession` calls `RoutePlanner.ensure()` when a route is started). The search index does not: `SearchOverlay` is composed on every launch and its poll calls `ui.refresh()`, which calls `PlaceSearch.ensure()`, so every pack's index is built at launch and within 2 s of a new pack whatever `recordArea` does; `PlaceSearch.ensure` splits into a read of the states that starts nothing and a build, and the overlay builds only while the search box is open or the query is non-blank (the states shown meanwhile say "not built yet" or build progress). The research (2026-10-27, `out/wave21-research/T149-graph-build.md` on the coordinator's PC): no surveyed app builds a routing graph on the phone from raw data; BRouter, OsmAnd, Organic Maps and Mapbox deliver a prebuilt structure the phone reads in pieces; this project is the outlier because packs carry no routing section, so building at first use is the right interim and a server-shipped graph is the field's answer for server-built packs (Later: a proposal and ADR, a pack-format change); no source estimates a build's heap from a header (measure, as below; margin about 1.5x; compare with the heap free now, not the maximum); the future streaming build partitions by `GraphTiler.ZOOM` tiles; no user setting. ### T156. Interrupted area downloads survive the background and the process: the WorkManager chain (after T151)
**Goal:** the resume idea's out-of-process half, per the research (2026-10-27, `out/wave21-research/T151-resume-downloads.md` on the coordinator's PC): one WorkManager unique one-time worker (`enqueueUniqueWork` with KEEP, a `CONNECTED` constraint, never `UNMETERED` (ADR 0023), exponential backoff) runs the tile loop from T151's cursor; a user's Download tap calls `setForeground` with the `dataSync` type and a notification with progress and Cancel; a background-started run wraps `setForeground` in try/catch and falls back to a plain job of about ten minutes; at Android 15's six-hours-per-day `dataSync` limit or a job stop it saves the cursor and re-enqueues itself; the chain survives a reboot through WorkManager's own persistence (a user force-stop cancels it, which nothing fixes, and the start trigger of T151 covers that case). No separate in-process network callback (the constraint is the connectivity trigger); no periodic run unless the device test shows the chain lost, then a 6 to 12 hour safety net that exists only while a record is unfinished. `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (declared already; `tracking/NotificationPrompt.kt` reused) asked on the first Download tap, not at launch; denial does not stop the service.
