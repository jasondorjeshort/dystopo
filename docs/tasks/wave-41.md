### T311. Flights on the map: live aircraft with origin and destination
**Goal:** a Flights layer on the map, off by default, that shows live aircraft in and around the view (the Horizon view's dots are T312). (1) **Source** (`shared/.../aircraft/`): an `AircraftSource` interface returning a list of aircraft (ICAO hex, call sign, registration, type, lat, lon, altitude, ground track, ground speed, vertical rate, position age); two implementations: the adsb.lol point-and-radius client (the finding cites `/v2/lat/{lat}/lon/{lon}/dist/{nm}`, the task text once said `/v2/point/...`: confirm the live path in the live poll; the centre is the **map view's centre** rounded to 0.1°, the radius from the view, at most 250 nm; parsing pure Kotlin in `commonMain`; the HTTP call through `online/OnlineFetcher.kt`'s `HttpGet`, a generic User-Agent `Dystopo/<version>` with no key and no device id) and a replay source reading a recorded JSON file (a synthetic flight set near the `rainier` area of `areas.json`, a test fixture). Altitude: geometric (WGS84 ellipsoid) converted to EGM2008 by the undulation measured at the phone (its fix's ellipsoid height minus `mslAltitudeMeters`, LocationSource.kt), applied to every aircraft of the poll; no fix or no geometric altitude: barometric, marked so. No geoid download. Between polls each aircraft is moved along its track by its speed and the position's age, capped at 30 s. Feed data lives in memory only: never written to disk, cached or exported (ODbL, the finding). (2) **Routes:** the CC0 route table (VRS standing data, call sign to the airports in order flown) and the airports (OurAirports, public domain: code, name, city, position), compacted by a new devtool command into one data file **committed** under `androidApp/src/main/assets/aircraft/` (create the folder) and refreshed by hand with that command; its format is internal to the app for now (the home site's refresh of it is a follow-up that takes an ADR first). Measure its size and report it; over 5 MB compressed ship airports only and say so. A route is shown only when plausible: the aircraft within a distance of the great circle between the leg's two airports that scales with the leg's length, and its track heading toward the next airport, rechecked every poll; otherwise the call sign alone and the card says "Route unknown"; a plausible route on the card carries "From schedule data; may be wrong". No call sign leaves the phone. (3) **Map:** each aircraft a silhouette turned to its track (drawn at run time with `style.addImage`, not the sprite sheet; shape, not colour alone, carries meaning), a short trail coloured by altitude (one fixed scale); labels (call sign, and "SEA > DEN" when plausible) only from a zoom threshold or under a count cap, dots always drawn; a tap selects one: its track since the layer saw it solid, the remaining great circle to the destination dashed when plausible, and the card. (4) **Card:** call sign, origin > destination as code and city, type and registration, altitude with climbing or descending, ground speed, distance, bearing and elevation angle from the phone, the position's age ("12 s ago"), and the credit "ADSB.lol contributors (ODbL)". (5) **States:** polls only in the foreground with the map showing; offline "No flight data: offline" (never an empty sky without a word); older than 30 s hollow with its age, older than 60 s dropped; a feed that stops "Flight data stopped"; HTTP 429 "Daily flight data limit reached" honouring Retry-After; the requests spent today counted on the phone (the UTC day) and "about N requests left today (UTC)" shown under the refresh setting (ADR 0023: no Wi-Fi gate). (6) **Settings** (ADR 0044, synced keys, a row, help text, a debug route each): Flight tracking (off; its help says the rounded centre of the map you are viewing goes to the feed named in the credits while it is on), refresh interval (20 s; 10, 20, 30, 60), airport names (codes on labels and both on the card by default; codes or cities). (8) **Added by the owner, 2026-11-20** (from the coordinator's review of the plan): altitude and speed in the user's unit preference (ADR 0022) with the flight level beside the altitude at cruise (from barometric altitude, above an 18,000 ft transition by default); aircraft on the ground hidden by default, shown from about z13, left out of labels and the count cap; emergency squawks 7700, 7600 and 7500 with a distinct ring (shape, not colour alone) and a card line ("Emergency", "Radio failure", "Hijack code"); silhouettes by the feed's emitter category (light aircraft, airliners by size, rotorcraft, glider, balloon, drone; the generic shape as fallback), with `squawk`, the emergency flag and `AircraftKind` in the shared model for T312. (7) **Registry:** `sources.json` entries for the live feed (ODbL-1.0, the credit on the map's attribution line while the layer draws), the route table (CC0-1.0) and the airports (public domain), with `provenance` on the layer's results.

### T312. Flights in the Horizon view: aircraft at their bearing and elevation angle
**Goal:** a fourth layer in the Horizon view, "Flights", with its toggle beside satellites, peaks and the sun (shown only while Flight tracking is on; off-by-default stays T311's setting). Each aircraft from T311's `AircraftSource` is a dot at its bearing and elevation angle from the phone (earth curvature included, T311's function), drawn hollow when it is hidden by terrain, meaning below `HorizonProfile.angleAt(bearing)` **and** farther than the ridge's `distanceM` at that bearing (a helicopter below the ridge line but nearer than the ridge is in sight), its angle computed from the same observer height as the profile (`groundM + observerHeightM`), or when its position is stale (T311's ages), with a 60 to 90 s tail and the label "call sign > destination code" (call sign alone without a plausible route). Labels: aircraft get their own `LabelKind` in `HorizonLabels`, placed in a **second pass**: `place(peaks)` runs unchanged, then `place(aircraft, blocked = the peaks' boxes and marks)` (the first pass forces the closest label and counts every bearing in its density gap, `HorizonLabels.kt:182-189`, so mixing would reshuffle peak names each poll); `HorizonUi.names` stays peaks-only for the tap hit-test. Aircraft on the ground are left out of the layer and the list; an emergency squawk gets a distinct ring on its dot and its row; rows show the aircraft's kind and altitudes in the user's units (T311's formatter; the owner's additions of 2026-11-20). A "Flights" list in the view, closest first like the Peaks list; a tap on a dot or a row opens T311's card, hosted inside `HorizonScreen` (T311's `FlightCard` composable; the Horizon view is drawn above the map, so the map's card would open underneath). The view carries the feed's credit "ADSB.lol contributors (ODbL)" while the layer draws (the view has no credit line today: a new line, its place stated in the report). In the Horizon view the poll's centre is the phone's position rounded to 0.1° (there is no map view), requested through T311's caller-driven poller while the view is open; T311's Flight tracking help already says so. The toggle is a new synced flag `horizonFlights` (default on; the toggle is shown only while Flight tracking is on), added to `HorizonLayers` and its parse and getter lines in `AppSettings.kt` (`horizonLayer()` and the `HorizonLayers(...)` getter, mid-file: T312 owns those lines). **Sky range** setting (ADR 0044; the finding recommends it because apps split): Overhead (above 45°, the finding), Near (within 50 km, a first guess), All (default, the finding), synced key, a row in the Horizon options, help text, a debug route.
