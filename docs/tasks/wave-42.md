### T314. Flight route lines: the path before the app saw the plane, and a setting for which flights show them
**Goal:** (1) **Before the app saw it:** for a flight with a plausible route, draw the great circle from its origin airport (`Match.origin`, the matched leg's previous airport) to the **first point of its kept history** (`history(hex).first()`, where the solid track starts, so dotted and solid always join; the tracker caps history at `HISTORY_MAX` and forgets an aircraft after `DROP_S`, so this point is "where the app's track starts", not "first ever seen"), dotted and lighter than the solid track (round caps, a dash pattern distinct from the route's `[2,2]`, e.g. `[0.5,2]`, lower opacity; style from the display research in `docs/findings/2026-11-20-aircraft-layer-sources-and-display.md` where it speaks, otherwise state the choice): a straight-line estimate that must read as a guess. Drawn only when that first point projects onto the current leg (along-track at or beyond minus the plausibility slack, as `RoutePlausibility.kt` does; so an A-B-A route whose history began on the earlier leg draws nothing backwards), and not when that first point is within 15 km of the origin (`GROUND_M`; the solid track covers it). The solid seen track and the dashed great circle from the current position to the destination stay as they are. Nothing is fetched and nothing new leaves the phone. The card gains one line, e.g. "Dotted: estimated path from SEA", and the card's help `flights.card` and the Flights section of `docs/reference/android-app.md` name the dotted estimate. (2) **Which flights show route lines** (ADR 0044: the owner asked for an option to compare): a synced setting `aircraftRouteLines`, "Flight tracking: Route lines", values `selected` (default, today's behaviour) and `all`; under All, every other flight with a plausible route gets one faint thin line origin > current position > destination (its solid track stays the selected flight's only; unselected flights have only their 90 s trail), the selected flight drawn as today on top. Draw cost: a line cap (50 by default, the flights nearest the view centre; state the figure's reason), points per line scaled with length (16 to 32), and the faint lines in their own GeoJSON source updated only on a poll, a selection or a setting change, not every second. A pure planner in `shared/.../aircraft/` decides which flights get which lines and applies the cap (tested in commonTest); the layer only draws its output. Row, help text, debug route, the next `AndroidControls` number after T311's rows. The Horizon view needs nothing new (no route lines there); say so in the report.
