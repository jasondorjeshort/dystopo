### T281. The drive search on directed-edge states, at zero turn cost (first)
**Goal:** the mechanism for the owner's bug (the idea above), with no behaviour change: the survey `out/wave33-research/drive-turn-search.md` [no-check: gitignored], points (a) to (d), read in full first, as corrected by the wave paragraph. A drive-only bidirectional A* (new `routing/DriveSearch.kt` [no-check: new file]) whose state in a base piece is a directed edge arrived at its head and in an overlay piece a border node's in or out label (shortcuts run in to out, the border crossing out to in); the potential is read at the head node; the start seeds both directions of its snapped edge, the goal is reached through the goal edge's predecessors, so the turn off the first edge and onto the last are both priced; the twin step at a piece border stops being a state (the twins' outgoing edges are offered directly). The turn function (new `routing/TurnCost.kt` [no-check: new file]) has its final signature (incoming edge, node, outgoing edge, the pieces, the driving side) and its geometry: bearings over `Directions.BEARING_M` from a `by lazy` cache counted in `approxBytes`; the count of distinct drivable roads at a node, twins included and parallel copies of one road counted once; a reversal read from geometry. Its table is all zero in this task, with a test-only non-zero table. `DriveOverlay`'s in-tile search and its unpack run the same edge search; level 2 is unchanged in code; no version change (costs are equal at zero). `Search` for hike and bike is not edited, and it stays able to plan a drive (the owner's option, the wave paragraph's point 6): a `driveTurns` parameter on `TileRouter` (default on) picks the search, and devtool `route --no-turns` and a `turns=0` parameter on the server's route request pass it. **Budget:** measure directed drive edges per drive node on the `rainier` and `montblanc` packs, set the drive search's state limit from that (the message keeps its sense), redefine the drive memory report (`searchBytes`), and state the phone's bytes at its limit. **The overlay on a small pack:** a `minDistanceM` parameter on `TileRouter` (default `DriveOverlay.MIN_DISTANCE_M`) and a devtool `route --overlay-min-km` switch, so the `rainier` pack's drives run through level 1.

### T284. Least fuel, the metric: the model, the search, the overlay's second cell file (after T283; ADR 0063)
**Goal:** the owner, 2026-11-14: least fuel beside least time and quicker planning (the wave paragraph). The engine half, reachable by devtool and the server only; T285 puts it on the phone. ADR 0063 fixes the model, read in full first with the survey `out/wave33-research/drive-least-fuel.md` [no-check: gitignored]; the second critic's points 1 to 4, 7, 10 and 11 are in it. **The model** (new `routing/FuelModel.kt` [no-check: new file]): fuel energy per directed edge from the ADR's formula and first values; it requires every unsteadiness and surface factor to be 1 or more and computes its own floor (the least joules per metre over all speeds with the descent credit at its cap), never a written constant; a turn's fuel is its idle over the turn's seconds plus the energy to regain speed, never under zero. **The metric:** `CostModel` takes a drive metric (time, fuel); a fuel cost is joules over the model's least flat joules per metre; the weight gains a direction (ascent and descent swap); the potential's factor is the floor over that divisor; a route's time stays the sum of its edges' times and turn seconds. The metric is a constructor property of `TileRouter`, so the `Router` interface and `model/RouteEditor.kt` are not edited. **The overlay:** the metric in the cell key and in `register`, `unregister`, `hasFile` and `cellsOf`; one LRU for both metrics; a fuel cell file `<vector>-overlay-fuel.idx` with the metric's id and a hash of the model's parameters in its header, refused and rebuilt when either differs; on the server the first fuel request queues one full background fuel warm-up (level 1, level 2, the file), and until it ends a fuel drive uses level-1 cells built on demand, as the phone always does; the time file and its cells are untouched. **Switches:** devtool `route --prefer fuel` and the server's `prefer=fuel` (T282's forms); the reply and the JSON name the metric beside `cost`, and carry `fuelIndex` (the route's fuel in the model's joules, for comparing routes only). The server's own editor operations and the browser demo plan least time (said in `docs/SERVER.md`).
**Owns:** shared `routing/Router.kt` (`CostModel`, the metric through `TileRouter`, the fields), `routing/DriveSearch.kt` and `routing/TurnCost.kt` (the cost of a turn through `CostModel`), `routing/DriveOverlay.kt`, the new `routing/FuelModel.kt` and `FuelModelTest.kt` [no-check: new files], `DriveRoutingTest.kt`, `DriveOverlayTest.kt`, `devtool/src/main/kotlin/io/github/jasondorjeshort/dystopo/devtool/RouteCommand.kt`, `serverlib/src/main/kotlin/io/github/jasondorjeshort/dystopo/server/RouteRoutes.kt` with `RouteRoutesTest.kt`, a committed synthetic fixture graph for devtool [no-check: new file], `docs/reference/devtool-commands.md`, `docs/SERVER.md`, the Routing section of `docs/PLANNING.md`. ### T286. Least fuel does not climb the mountain a tunnel passes under (after T284, before T285; small)
**Goal:** found by T284: a drive edge's ascent and descent are read from the terrain along it, so a tunnel is priced as a climb over the ground above (Les Houches to Entreves reads 3,136 m of ascent through its tunnel) and a bridge as the descent into the valley it spans. Under least fuel that is a wrong figure on the phone's percentage and a wrong choice wherever a tunnel competes with a road over a pass. The graph marks both (`RoutingGraph`'s bridge and tunnel bit, T89). Under the fuel metric a marked edge is priced on the straight grade between its two ends' heights: ascent the rise from end to end if positive, descent the fall, nothing between; a marked edge cut at a tile border keeps the same rule per piece (said in the KDoc as a known coarseness: the cut point's height is the terrain's). The route's reported ascent for a drive follows the same rule, so the summary and the fuel agree. Least time and quick routes must not change (the drive time does not read ascent; the report confirms it or names where it does). Hike and bike are not touched: a foot tunnel's profile is a separate question, recorded as a follow-up if seen. The fuel cell file's version goes to 2, since its cells' costs change.
**Owns:** shared `routing/Router.kt` (the fuel pricing of a marked edge and the drive's reported ascent), `routing/FuelModel.kt` only if the rule needs a line there, `routing/DriveOverlay.kt` (the fuel file's version), `DriveRoutingTest.kt`, `DriveOverlayTest.kt`, `FuelModelTest.kt`, `docs/reference/devtool-commands.md` and `docs/SERVER.md` where a figure or the version is named, the Routing section of `docs/PLANNING.md`.
**Interacts with:** T284 (merged first): `CostModel.partCost`, the fuel file's header. T285 (after this merges): `fuelIndex` and the percentage read these costs.
**Done when:** `check` green with a test of a tunnel under a 400 m hill beside a road over it (least fuel takes the tunnel and its reported ascent is the ends' rise; without the mark it takes neither figure), a bridge over a valley, and a marked edge driven in both directions; a version 1 fuel cell file refused and rebuilt; with devtool `route`, Les Houches to Entreves under `--prefer fuel` and `time` before and after (ascent, `fuelIndex`, the route; the time route's distance and time unchanged) and the other five public drives under the three values, time and quick byte-equal to `main` but for the ascent field if it changes, each change in a fuel figure explained by a marked edge; the count of marked drive edges on the `rainier` and `montblanc` packs.
