### T202. The shipped graph is there before the first route: the server builds every pack's missing graph at start and on refresh
**Goal:** `docs/ideas/2026-10-31-server-lists-no-graph-until-a-route-is-planned.md` (deleted at the merge). Today `PackCatalog.graph` lists a `derived` graph only when `<vector archive>-graph.idx` exists beside the pack and matches it, and that file is written by the build job's `graph` phase (T189) or by the first route (`RouteRoutes.openOrBuild`), so a pack built by `tilebuild` or `devtool tiles` and copied into `out/` ships no graph until someone routes there, and the first phones build their own, the slow path ADR 0049 set out to remove. Change: the catalog's warm thread (`PackCatalog.warm`, after its hashing; `devtool serve --live` gains the same call the standalone server's `Main.kt` makes) and `POST /catalog/refresh` build the graph of every pack pair whose graph is missing or stale (`graphProblem` non-null), one pack at a time, newest pack first, through the same `RoutePlanner` the routes and the build phase use (so a graph is never built twice and a route arriving meanwhile waits on the same lock), writing the file and its `.sha256` sidecar; the catalog lists it as soon as the file is in place. Docs: `docs/SERVER.md` (the catalog and What to monitor sections), `docs/reference/live-server.md`, the KDoc lines of `PackCatalog.kt` and `BuildRoutes.kt` that say the file comes from the phase or the first route.

### T203. A position from the terrain when the GPS is gone: the live source on the phone
**Goal:** the `Plan:` of `docs/ideas/2026-10-29-terrain-matched-position-without-gps.md` (deleted at the merge; the measurement is T166's `shared/.../nav/TerrainFix.kt`, `devtool terrain-fix` and finding `docs/findings/2026-10-30-terrain-matched-position-measurement.md`). **Pass 1 (this task; the critic cut the step detector and its `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION` permission prompt to pass 2, Later):** a terrain source in `androidApp` (`tracking/TerrainSource.kt`, new) reads `TYPE_PRESSURE` (through a new `TERRAIN` reason in `Barometer.kt`) and the heading (`controls/Heading.kt`), takes the stride rate from the recording's last good minutes (the pace before the fixes went), feeds the shared `TerrainFix` and enters `tracking/` through `LocationSource` as one more source with a quality mark (`source: terrain`, the spread as the accuracy). It is seeded from the last good fix and its course and re-seeded at every good fix; it runs only while the GPS has reported poor accuracy (the activity's bound) or no fix for a minute, under the T150 background rule; the heading term is used only while the phone's orientation is steady against the direction of travel, otherwise dropped; it reports no fix once the spread passes 300 m (the finding's fan-out). The track records the source. A phone without a barometer gets no terrain fix, said in the diagnostics (`settings/Diagnostics.kt`, one line). **A terrain fix never reaches the readout or the course:** `MapController.onFix` hands every shown fix to `Heading.onFix` and `LiveReadout.onMapFix`; a fix with `source: terrain` is drawn by the puck and recorded, but is not passed to the readout (its DEM height at the estimated place would feed T204's estimator a circular height) nor to `Heading`'s course (the heading term would consume its own output). **Survey (2026-11-01, the report in the wave paragraph):** no app with a public source documents a sensor-estimated position as its own state, a start delay or a give-up distance; the common practice is an accuracy ring whose radius is the accuracy and, for "no fix", a puck colour or style change that stays on the last known position (one app a dashed ring when unfixed, another a grey dot). So: a setting (ADR 0044: no practice, a wrong dot on a trail hurts some users, the sensors cost battery), "Estimate position when GPS is poor", on by default, help text saying it needs a barometer and a recent good fix; while estimating the puck is a hollow dot with the existing outline and a dashed ring at the filter's spread (never smaller than the honest spread), the heading cone only while the heading term is in use, a chip "Estimated position, about 80 m"; after the give-up a grey puck on the last estimate with "No position, last known 12 min ago"; the track source is named "estimated" in user-visible text (never "dead reckoning" or "terrain-matched"); the 60 s start and 300 m cap are the project's own and not user-tunable. Estimated points are recorded with their source and the flagged-fix mark and left out of distance totals until the next good fix (ADR 0033: said in the report for the owner). Debug: `POST /location/terrain` runs a replay log (`tracking/Replay.kt`'s file with a pressure column; no public GPX carries one, so the implementer synthesises the column from the DEM along a public Rainier GPX, as the finding did) through the live source and `GET /location/terrain` returns the estimates as JSON (seeded at, particles, spread, state, the no-fix reason). Out of scope: off-trail (the grid variant), the step detector (pass 2), a third pass if the numbers fall short.
