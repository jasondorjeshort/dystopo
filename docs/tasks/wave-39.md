### T308. The Horizon view: the square sky, layer toggles for Satellites, Peaks and Sun
**Goal:** the GPS view (T307, `app/gnss/`) becomes the **Horizon view**. Layout (the owner, 2026-11-19): "the horizon itself as being a square (with circle inside) locked onto the top or left of the 16:9 or 4:3 phone view, with everything else being a scrollable area at the bottom to control it": in portrait the square plot is fixed at the top, full width; in landscape fixed at the left, full height; the rest of the screen scrolls (the toggles, then each layer's own content). The plot keeps the land's skyline, the rings, N E S W and the orientation setting. **Layer toggles** (one row of toggle buttons at the top of the scrolling area; each remembered across launches the way the map's own layer choices are, not a new synced setting unless the critic shows the app syncs those): (1) **Satellites**: T307's satellites on the plot, and in the scroll area the summary, Check my GPS and the list; off, the GNSS listener is not held. (2) **Peaks**: the named peaks of the active pack that stand on the skyline, labelled at their bearing on the plot (`shared/.../dem/Viewshed.kt`'s `peaks` gives bearing, apparent angle and the skyline flag; reuse the plot's own `HorizonProfile`, no second sweep where it can be avoided), the highest few per sector of the rim when crowded, the full list (name, height, distance, bearing) in the scroll area. (3) **Sun**: the sun now and today's path across the plot (`shared/.../nav/Sun.kt`'s `position` over the day), with the times it goes behind the terrain and comes out again, and sunrise and sunset over a flat horizon for comparison, in the scroll area; the terrain times agree with the turn-around clock's `terrainSunsetMs` for the same point and day. Defaults: all three on. Names in the UI: "Horizon view" for the screen; the Settings row "GPS status ›" becomes "Horizon view ›" in the same place (the map's switch comes later). The idea files `2026-11-19-peak-names-on-the-sky-plots-skyline.md` and `2026-11-19-todays-sun-path-across-the-skyline.md` are built by this task and deleted on the branch; the Horizon view idea stays (its switch and its later layers).
Resolved for the pass (the coordinator, from the critic of 2026-11-19): (a) the package `app/gnss/` keeps its name and its `/gnss*` routes for the satellites; the new pieces go in a new `app/horizon/` with `GET|POST /horizon` (the layers, the peaks, the sun's path and times, a `date=` parameter as `/shade/sun` has) and `POST /horizon/ui?open=1|0`. (b) The toggles are synced like the map's layers (`MapOptions.KEYS`, `sync/SettingsSync.kt`): three keys in the `AppSettings` registry, so `SettingsRegistryTest`'s count, `laterKeys` and `seed` change; the report lists the keys (ADR 0033). (c) The sun's terrain times come from `Sun.onTerrain(lat, lon, y, m, d, profile::angleAt)` on the plot's own profile, the call the turn-around clock reaches through `ShadeTiles.pointAnswer`; nothing is computed twice. The times shown are the upper limb's, as the turn-around clock's are; the arc is drawn at the sun's centre (about a minute apart at a crossing). (d) Peaks: an overload of `Viewshed.peaks` taking the `HorizonProfile` (in `dem/Viewshed.kt`, named in the report) and the sweep's `Terrain` kept beside the profile; peaks read with `Viewshed.readPeaks` as `ViewshedSession` does; the plot's 30 km range is said in the help text. (e) With Satellites off the view follows the map's own fix; the skyline, peaks and sun move after `POST /location`. (f) Alpha choices (ADR 0031), stated in the report: the toggles a row of toggle buttons (the owner's words; the survey found apps use a settings sheet, which the owner's layout replaces); on the rim at most one peak name per 30° sector, the highest, the rest in the list, a tap on a label or a row showing name, height, distance and bearing. (g) The square is `min(width, height)` of the window, at the top in portrait and the left in landscape. (h) Every help text, summary and setting text that says "GPS status" says "Horizon view" (`SettingsPages.kt`, `SettingsLayout.kt`'s page summary, `GnssText.kt`, the in-view help); the row's id `gnssStatus` stays, its label changes. (i) The Horizon view's section of `docs/reference/android-app.md` (over 300 lines) moves to a new `docs/reference/android-horizon-view.md` with its line in `docs/reference/README.md` and a pointer left behind.
