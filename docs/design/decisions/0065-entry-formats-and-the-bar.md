# 0065, sibling: the entry formats and the rising bar

The formats and the table [ADR 0065](0065-coordinator-brainstorms-offered-on-yes-with-a-rest-pile.md)
decides. Changing a format here is a change to that ADR.

## The offer's header

One line above the numbered list, for example:

```
Queue 172 open (at the session's start), step 5: each shows its lever and the open idea it
outranks. 2 near-term, 2 intermediate, 3 moonshots; 2 recurrences; held back: 4, ask to see them.
```

The list follows: at most nine lines, grouped near-term, intermediate, moonshot, one or two lines
each naming the anchor. The owner answers by number: record, record at rest, fold into a named
file, or no. "Record" and "record at rest" are offered side by side; neither is pre-selected.

## The Source line

The first body line after the H1 of an idea file (before the H1, the listing would read it as the
title):

```
Source: coordinator, generated by claude-sonnet-5-5 and recorded by claude-opus-5-5, after the
triage of 2026-11-22; offered and accepted by the owner; not sanity-checked. Moonshot.
```

- The model that generated the idea comes from the agent's own model id or the `Co-Authored-By`
  trailer of the commit that brought it; when the recorder differs, both are named; when they are
  the same, `coordinator (<model id>)`.
- The trigger is named in public terms only (a triage, a wave close, a release, a spark during
  named public work).
- `Moonshot.` closes the line when the idea is one; a `(moonshot)` suffix on the title is allowed.
- The owner's own ideas read `Source: the owner, <context>`.

## The recurrence entry

A dated paragraph above the file's block (at the end when it has none):

```
Came up again: 2026-12-03, coordinator (claude-opus-5-5), at the close of wave 47, while reading
the merged heading correction. New: the manual correction seeds the camera match's bearing, so the
search narrows to plus or minus 15 degrees.
```

- Required: the date, the source with its model, the context.
- `New:` is optional: a new angle, scope, anchor or constraint.
- Counting: one per distinct session or source (the owner, another session, another model, a
  report). An entry written in the same session as a triage that read the file is kept for its
  `New:` and does not count. Counted by `grep -c "^Came up again:"` and reading the sources.

## The use line

When one primitive is offered as several features, the uses go on the primitive's file:

```
Use: 2026-11-22, coordinator (claude-opus-5-5): a coverage panel under the status chip, the first
visible reader of this function (accepted by the owner).
```

A landing pass removes the `Use:` and `New:` lines it built; the file stays while any unbuilt one
remains.

## The rest prefix

```
Waits: at rest — "keep it, later" (the owner, 2026-11-22)
```

The quoted text is the owner's own words or the text of the option they picked, never the
coordinator's paraphrase. Found by `grep "^Waits: at rest"`.

## The rising bar

One step per doubling of the open count, read once at the session's start (every file the listing
prints, resting ideas included). Guidance only: it never refuses, a step may lag a session when a
wave lands ideas near a boundary, and an offer below the bar is allowed with a stated reason.

| Open ideas | What an offered idea also shows |
|---|---|
| under 20 | an anchor in the tree |
| 20 to 40 | no existing file says it (else it is offered as a recurrence entry) |
| 40 to 80 | it is not one use of a primitive another file or this list names (else a `Use:` line) |
| 80 to 160 | its lever: what it unblocks, which report or correction it answers, or why a moonshot is worth carrying |
| 160 to 320 | which open idea of its kind it outranks, judged on the shortlist only, one line of reason each |
| 320 and up | the owner asked for its topic, or it recurred from a distinct source |

The coordinator judges at the offer, the owner at acceptance, triage afterwards. An idea below the
bar is never dropped: it is offered as a `Use:` line or a recurrence entry on its home file, or
counted in the header's held-back number.
